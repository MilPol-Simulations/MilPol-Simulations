W-3WARM Anakonda / A modernised maritime rescue platform

An upgraded Anakonda variant that retains the maritime search-and-rescue mission while introducing a modernised flight deck and updated operational equipment.

WHAT SETS THIS VARIANT APART

Updated flight deck

Modernised displays and avionics create a different cockpit workflow from the earlier W-3RM.

Rescue mission retained

Maritime rescue equipment and the operational demands of over-water SAR remain central.

Equipment evolution

The modernisation is represented as a distinct aircraft configuration, not merely a new livery.

MilPol development scope

The W-3WARM is planned as a later add-on alongside the W-3PL. Precise equipment differences will be refined against airframe-specific references.
