OUR APPROACH

DETAIL WITH PURPOSE.

We aim to recreate aircraft as coherent machines, not simply exterior models. That means researching their construction, cockpit layouts, equipment and operating characteristics, then developing those elements step by step.

RESEARCH FIRST

Available photographs, technical references and future first-hand recordings inform our work. Where references are incomplete, we continue researching rather than presenting assumptions as confirmed facts.

PROGRESS, NOT PROMISES

We share work in progress and distinguish planned features from completed systems. Our aim is to publish meaningful development updates approximately every two months, when progress allows.

INDEPENDENT DEVELOPMENT

Built by a small independent team, with an emphasis on careful modelling, research and systems work.

OUR FIRST PROGRAMME

THE W-3 SOKÓŁ FAMILY.

The initial package is planned to include the analogue W-3 and W-3RM Anakonda. The W-3PL Głuszec and W-3WARM Anakonda are planned as a later expansion. All variants remain in development; specifications and release plans may evolve.
