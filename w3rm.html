AIRCRAFT TECHNICAL PROFILE

W-3RM Anakonda / Rescue at sea

The Polish Naval Aviation search-and-rescue adaptation of the Sokół, developed around over-water operations and the recovery of people in distress.

RoleMaritime search and rescue
PowerplantTwo PZL-10W turboshaft engines
RotorFour-blade main rotor
Specialist equipmentRescue hoist and maritime emergency equipment; fit varies

WHAT SETS THIS VARIANT APART

Rescue-oriented equipment

A hoist and rescue cabin arrangements distinguish the Anakonda from a utility transport Sokół.

Maritime protection

Emergency flotation equipment and over-water operational provisions are defining features of the type.

Search-and-rescue workflow

The mission emphasises locating casualties, hovering and supporting rescue crew operations.

MilPol development scope

The W-3RM is planned alongside the baseline W-3 in the initial package. Individual mission equipment will be modelled according to verified references.
