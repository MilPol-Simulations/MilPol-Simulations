AIRCRAFT TECHNICAL PROFILE

W-3 Sokół / The adaptable foundation

A Polish twin-engine, four-bladed helicopter built for utility flying, passenger transport and specialist missions. The baseline Sokół is the starting point for the wider W-3 family.

RoleUtility, transport and multi-role operations
PowerplantTwo PZL-10W turboshaft engines
RotorFour-blade main rotor
Flight crewTypically two pilots; cabin arrangements vary

WHAT SETS THIS VARIANT APART

Analogue flight deck

Traditional flight and engine instruments provide the classic Sokół cockpit experience.

Configurable airframe

Different nose arrangements, lighting positions and mission fittings distinguish real W-3 configurations.

Multi-role cabin

The aircraft can be configured around transport and specialist tasks rather than one fixed mission.

MilPol development scope

The initial package is planned to include the baseline W-3 and W-3RM. The W-3 is intended to offer distinct exterior configurations where reference material supports them.
