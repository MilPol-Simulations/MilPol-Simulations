FAQ & SUPPORT

Answers to common questions about MilPol Simulations, the W-3 project and your account.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is MilPol Simulations?

MilPol Simulations is a small independent team developing aircraft for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024. Our first project is the PZL W-3 Sokół family.

Which W-3 variants are planned?

The initial package is planned to include the analogue W-3 and W-3RM Anakonda. The W-3PL Głuszec and W-3WARM Anakonda are planned for a later expansion. Plans may change.

When will the W-3 be released?

There is no confirmed release date. We will announce timing when development and testing are sufficiently advanced.

Will the aircraft work in MSFS 2020?

The current project targets Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024. We have not announced an MSFS 2020 version.

How often are development updates published?

We aim to share meaningful development updates approximately every two months, depending on progress.

Can I vote on the next MilPol project?

Yes. Visit What Comes Next? and sign in to your MilPol account to vote for a potential future aircraft. The poll is advisory, not a promise of a future release.

How can I report an issue or contact the team?

You can contact the community team through the official MilPol Discord or Instagram links below. Please avoid posting passwords or private account details publicly.

Where can I manage my account?

Use the Sign In or Account link in the navigation. For account access problems, use the password-reset option on the sign-in page.
