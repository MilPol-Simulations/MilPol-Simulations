AIRCRAFT TECHNICAL PROFILE

W-3PL Głuszec / Modernised for the battlefield

A substantially modernised Polish Army W-3 variant, designed for battlefield support with an upgraded cockpit and specialised mission equipment.

RoleBattlefield support and armed utility
PowerplantTwin PZL-10W-family turboshaft engines
RotorFour-blade main rotor
AvionicsModernised integrated cockpit with multifunction displays

WHAT SETS THIS VARIANT APART

Digital cockpit

Multifunction displays and a reworked instrument layout set the Głuszec apart from the analogue Sokół.

Mission systems

Military communications, observation and self-protection equipment reflect its battlefield-support role; fit varies.

Distinct configuration

Its cockpit and mission environment require more than a repaint of the baseline helicopter.

MilPol development scope

The Głuszec is planned as a later add-on. Detailed simulation of individual mission systems remains subject to development scope and available references.
